Yi et al. paper:
    "Sampling-Bias-Corrected Neural Modeling for Large-Scale Item Recommendations" (Yi et al., Google RecSys 2019)
    (implemented in this project's TwoTowerDNN)
    - the in-batch softmax is the computationally feasible replacement
      for a full softmax over all candidate items:  in a mini-batch,
      the items which are not the item of consideration act as negatives.
      - without a correction: popular items appear much
        more often in the negatives which inflates their loss and
        penalizes their embeddings.
      - the log-Q correction of Yi et al. tracks the items sampling frequency
        and calculates an item probability from it, takes the log of that
        (called logQ) and subtracts it from the logits:
            logits = matmul(user_embedding, movie_embedding) / temperature
            log_q = log( p_movie )
            logits -= (beta_correction * log_q
        then softmax on these corrected logits leads to unbiased gradients
        and a true multinomial full-softmax distribution.
           s_corrected(user_i, movie_j) = s(user_i, movie_j) - logQ(movie_j)
