   The main h-parameters affecting the mebeddings are
       weight_decay = 0.01, bias_corr_alpha = 0.05, drop_rate = 0.25

   Note that the User and Movie Models were changed to removed the

   normalization, bias, gamma and beta layers.

A look through the movie embeddings t-SNE and Umap plots and the
same for the user embeddings:
- underfitting:
  DNN[8,4] and DNN[16,8]
  maps show that the user hypershpere collapses into a hollow ring
  because there are not enough dimensions to represent the features
  well without wrapping around the ring.
  (this effect is even more obvious when a high weight_Decay of 0.05
   is used and a bias_corr_alpha of 0.01 is used)
  
- overfitting:
  DNN[128,64] and DNN[256, 128]
  maps show that the embeddings are not evenly filling the space,
  but ratther are highly fractured and separated as clumps
  and that the tiers are no co-spatial.

- contenders:
  DNN[16,32] and DNN[64,32] fill the embedding space relatively well, 
  especially considering that movie features are only id and genres
  at this point.  the tiers are co-spatial.
  one would expect good ability to perform ANN searches using these
  embeddings.

This states that a business goal pursuing accurate prediction of popularity
cannot predict future well because of the covariate shift.  
Popularity is non-stationary.

A business goal focusing on P(Y|X) is stable.
Personalization and latent preferences should be fine to use.
P(Y=1 | user_context, item_features) remains stable, for instance.
